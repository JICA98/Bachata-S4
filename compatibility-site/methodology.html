Bachata S4

How compatibility testing works

Reports are useful only when the environment that produced them is recorded.

One report, one test

Each confirmed test records the CUSA ID, Bachata S4 release, device, Android version, graphics driver, status, performance data, notes, and available screenshots or logs.

Status meanings

Playable means suitable for normal play in the tested configuration. Ingame means controllable gameplay was reached but major issues may remain. Menus means the title reaches menus but not gameplay. Boots means the title starts and produces meaningful output before an early blocker. Nothing means it crashes, hangs, or produces no useful output.

History stays visible

A newer report does not erase an older test. The game page shows the complete report history so regressions, improvements, device differences, and driver differences remain visible.
