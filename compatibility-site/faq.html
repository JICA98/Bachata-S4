Bachata S4

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about the project and compatibility database.

Does Bachata S4 include games?

No. Bachata S4 does not distribute PS4 games, firmware, licenses, decryption keys, or other copyrighted console files.

Does Ingame mean fully playable?

No. Ingame only means controllable gameplay was reached in at least one recorded configuration. Stability, completion, rendering, audio, input, or performance problems may remain.

Why can two phones behave differently?

CPU generation, GPU, available memory, Android version, thermal limits, graphics driver, emulator release, and game update version can all affect the result.

Where are compatibility reports stored?

Structured test reports, screenshots, and logs are maintained in the public JICA98/Bachata-S4-Compatibility repository and are assembled into this site during deployment.
