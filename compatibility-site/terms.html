Compatibility statuses are observational

Compatibility statuses shown on this website (Playable, Ingame, Menus, Boots, Nothing) describe what a tester observed for a specific emulator release, device, Android version, and graphics driver. They are not guarantees that a game will behave the same way for you, on your device, or on any future release of Bachata S4.

Always check the report details — release, device, driver, notes, and known issues — before drawing conclusions. Emulator projects change quickly, and results can improve or regress between releases.
