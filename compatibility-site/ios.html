    How to read these numbers

    Avg is frames shown divided by seconds. 1% low and 0.1% low are the fps of the slowest 1% and 0.1% of frames. All three cover gameplay only; stalls caused by our own test screenshots are excluded. The line on each bar marks the 30 fps goal on a 0–60 scale. A striped bar is a menu number, not gameplay. Frames land on 60 Hz steps, so lows read 60, 30, 20, 15 or 12.

    Results are specific to the tested device and build and may differ on yours. Bachata S4 does not distribute games. Use only software you are legally entitled to use.
