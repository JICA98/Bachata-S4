Privacy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Overview

Bachata S4 is a public compatibility and project-information website. You do not need an account to browse it, and the site does not intentionally request personal information through registration or account forms. Technical compatibility reports are public by design because their purpose is to document reproducible emulator behavior.

Hosting and basic request information

The website is deployed using GitHub Pages. As with normal web hosting, the hosting provider may process information required to deliver pages and protect the service, such as requested URLs, timestamps, IP addresses, user-agent strings, and network/security data. GitHub's handling of that information is governed by GitHub's own privacy documentation.

Compatibility reports and evidence

Published reports can include a game title and CUSA serial, Bachata S4 release and commit, device label, SoC, GPU, Android version, graphics driver, emulator settings, performance measurements, test date, tester-chosen public handle, screenshots, and sanitized logs. Contributors should remove private tokens, account identifiers, personal paths, or other unnecessary personal information before submitting logs or screenshots.

Local browser storage

The site may use local browser storage for small user-interface preferences such as the selected light or dark theme. That preference remains in the browser unless you clear site data. The compatibility filters can also exist temporarily in the page URL so a filtered view can be shared.

Google AdSense and advertising

The site is configured for Google AdSense publisher account pub-8704776489115590. When Google advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers, IP-derived information, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, limit, and personalize advertising as permitted by applicable law and your consent choices.

For regions where Google requires a certified consent-management platform for personalized or non-personalized advertising, the site owner should use Google's Privacy & Messaging tools or another Google-certified CMP rather than a custom consent banner that does not integrate with Google's consent requirements.

Third-party links

The site links to GitHub repositories, issues, release pages, Google Play, upstream emulator projects, and other external resources. Following those links leaves bachatas4.games. The destination site controls its own cookies, logs, accounts, and privacy practices.

Contact and corrections

Privacy questions, compatibility-data corrections, or requests to remove accidentally submitted personal information can be raised through the contact routes. Because compatibility evidence is also stored in public Git history, a correction may require both a website-data change and a repository history review depending on the material involved.
