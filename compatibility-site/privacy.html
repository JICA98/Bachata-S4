BachataS4 Emulator for iPhone and iPad

This section covers the BachataS4 Emulator app on the App Store (bundle ID games.bachatas4).

Data the app collects: none. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash upload, and it sends nothing about you or your device to the project or to anyone else.

The games and other software you add, their saved data, your settings and the diagnostic logs stay on your device, inside the app's storage. Logs are written to the app's folder in the Files app so that you can read or share them yourself; the app never sends them anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

The emulator's online features are off and the app has no setting to turn them on. The app does not connect to the project's servers or to this website.

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions can be asked on the issue tracker.
